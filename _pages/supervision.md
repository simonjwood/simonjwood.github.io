Prospective PhD students
-----
If you are interested in doing a PhD with me, please email me **before** applying. This lets us discuss whether a project with me suits you and which funding route fits your situation. Please do not apply through the Cardiff PhD portal naming me as supervisor without contacting me first. I do not currently have any funded open positions (as of October 2026). When I do, they are advertised on [FindAPhD](https://www.findaphd.com), but even then, **please email me first** rather than applying directly.

When you write, include **a brief description of your research interests, any past relevant experience and why you are interested in working with me specifically**. Due to the large volume of emails I receive, I will only respond to emails which follow these instructions.

PhD projects supervised by me will usually be on some mixture of vertex algebras, conformal field theory and tensor categories. A strong background in algebra (Lie algebras, representation theory) and some mathematical physics is ideal. Good books to get a feel for these topics include:

* [Vertex algebras and algebraic curves](https://doi.org/10.1090/surv/088) by Frenkel and Ben-Zvi
* [Lectures on tensor categories and modular functors](https://doi.org/10.1090/ulect/021) by Bakalov and Kirillov
* [Conformal Field Theory](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-2256-9) by Di Francesco, Mathieu and Sénéchal

I am a member of the [Geometry, Algebra, Mathematical Physics and Topology (GAPT) Research Group](https://www.cardiff.ac.uk/research/explore/research-units/geometry-algebra-mathematical-physics-and-topology) where we offer a lively community, and organise many seminars and conferences.

Postdoctoral fellowships
-----
Postdoctoral researchers interested in applying for fellowships (e.g. EPSRC, Marie Skłodowska-Curie, Royal Society, Leverhulme) to work with me are also welcome to get in touch. Please contact me at least three months before the relevant deadline, so we have time to prepare a strong application together. As a Humboldt alumnus, I can also host [Feodor Lynen Research Fellowships](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/feodor-lynen-research-fellowship) for researchers from Germany (German citizens, or researchers currently working in Germany who plan to return there).

Summer projects for Cardiff students
-----
Students enrolled at Cardiff University can do summer projects with me. If you are interested in one, let me know early because proposals usually need to be submitted in January for the following summer. Projects also need to be advertised to the entire university and awarded competitively.
